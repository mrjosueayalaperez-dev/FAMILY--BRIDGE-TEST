📍 Person Locator

Find where a person is being held, using only official government locator systems. This guide never asks for or keeps anyone's name — you type it only on the official government page, never here.

🎙 Prepare the search by voice

Tap the microphone and say “Find Juan Rodriguez” — or say an A-Number digit by digit. The app fills the boxes, reads them back, and waits for your OK. You can also type the details yourself. Nothing you say or type here is kept, stored, or sent anywhere by this app — after you confirm, you enter the details yourself on the official government page.

Note: your phone’s voice service may process your speech to turn it into text. Family Bridge does not send your name or A-Number to its own servers or any database — it has none.

🇺🇸 ICE Detainee Locator — immigration detention

    Names must match exactly (a hyphenated last name needs its hyphen). Records of people under 18 cannot be searched. If nothing is found, the record may not have appeared yet — try again later or ask an attorney.

    ↗ Open the official ICE locator

    🏛 Federal Prison Locator (BOP) — federal inmates

      This system covers federal prisoners only — not state, county, or immigration detention. A release date shown there can change; the Bureau says to check back.

      ↗ Open the official BOP locator

      For a state or county jail there is no single national system. Use that state's official corrections website — and check that it is truly the government's site before typing a name anywhere.

      Court case status is a different tool (the EOIR case system). It shows court information only — it does not say where a person is held, so it is not part of this locator.
